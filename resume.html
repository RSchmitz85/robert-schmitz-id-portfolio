Professional summary

Instructional designer and doctoral student in Curriculum and Instruction (Instructional Design) at the University of Central Florida, with 10+ years building learning systems end to end: standards-to-objective curriculum maps, modular Canvas architecture, assessment item banks built as reusable products, QTI packaging for LMS import, and facilitator-led training that puts finished systems in other instructors' hands. Designs for adult and secondary learners alike, in any content domain: analyze, write the objective, design the evidence, then build the instruction.

10,000+ standards-aligned assessment items authored into reusable Canvas item banks · Canvas course package adopted district-wide and delivered by other instructors · 10+ years designing, delivering, and revising learning programs.

Education & credentials

  • Ed.D. in progress — Curriculum and Instruction (Instructional Design) University of Central Florida
  • M.Ed., Mathematics Education (4.0 GPA) University of Central Florida
  • B.S., Social Studies Education University of South Florida
  • Professional Certification in Instructional Design University of Central Florida

Experience highlights

  • Authored 10,000+ items aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. standards, organized into Canvas item banks that return instant feedback and generate on-demand practice
  • Built and maintains the modular Canvas architecture behind multiple secondary courses — sequenced modules, embedded assessment, study guides, and instructional video
  • Canvas course package adopted district-wide and delivered by instructors who did not build it
  • 11+ years teaching secondary and adult mathematics, including Algebra 1, Geometry, remedial/intensive courses, and adult GED
  • Lead teacher, mentor, and district PD facilitator — curriculum design, standards implementation, Canvas course development
  • Contributed items and blueprints to district progress-monitoring assessments; Florida B.E.S.T. standards liaison
