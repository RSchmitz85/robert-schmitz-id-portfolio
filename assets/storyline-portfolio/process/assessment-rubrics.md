# Assessment rubrics

Executable reference tests check design rules only. Native Storyline assessment is not implemented or verified.

## Service Recovery

Five fixed 0–2 scores; pass at 8/10 plus no unresolved unauthorized promise and a named owner, channel and update time. Corrections repair promises without restoring earlier points.

### acknowledgment

| Choice | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 1 | A general apology recognizes frustration but does not address the expectation your team created. |
| B | 2 | Acknowledges the specific impact, accepts ownership of the expectation and moves toward evidence. |
| C | 0 | Shifts the work back to the customer without acknowledging the impact or taking ownership. |

### diagnosis

| Choice | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 2 | Uses current evidence, identifies the earlier unverified expectation and establishes the customer’s actual constraint. |
| B | 1 | Confirms current status but misses the earlier note and customer preference. |
| C | 0 | Relies on an old reassurance instead of the current carrier evidence. |

### policy fit

| Choice | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 1 | The trace is authorized, but the response withholds the available shipping refund and gives a narrow set of options. |
| B | 0 | Creates an unverified delivery promise. Marking a trace urgent cannot guarantee arrival. |
| C | 2 | Offers concrete authorized actions and separates them from an unverified delivery date. |

### escalation judgment

| Choice | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 1 | Correctly states personal authority but omits the available lead-review route. |
| B | 2 | Uses appropriate escalation, separates review from approval, and retains ownership. Escalation earns full credit. |
| C | 0 | Promises compensation beyond the representative’s authority. |

### follow-through

| Choice | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 1 | Offers a channel but no specific update time. Any earlier inaccurate promise remains unresolved. |
| B | 0 | Transfers ownership and gives no follow-up commitment. Any earlier inaccurate promise remains unresolved. |
| C | 2 | Confirms an authorized plan, retains ownership and commits to a specific update. Includes any correction that the earlier conversation requires. |

## Support Ticket

| Criterion | Points | Observable work |
|---|---:|---|
| Priority | 20 | P2: team blocked, no workaround, cutoff in 45 minutes |
| Knowledge | 20 | KB-202 matches online LP-07 and identified stuck job J-883 |
| Approved action | 20 | Remove J-883 only; resume LP-07; request one TEST label |
| Documentation | 20 | Four accurate structured fields agree with the recorded action and fresh confirmation |
| Verified closure | 20 | Inspect successful employee confirmation after the latest approved action; then save Resolved |

Pass requires at least 80 and the approved-action and verified-closure criteria. A wrong priority or article can yield an 80-point pass; an unapproved action or missing verified closure cannot. Guided practice adds no challenge points. Optional free text is unscored. Review reads a frozen snapshot; retry resets all fields and event order.
