NORTHLINE LOGISTICS / RELAY DESK
Resolve the ticket.
Verify the work.
Use evidence to prioritize, act, document, and close.
Priority matrix
P1 / Critical
Whole site stopped; no usable alternative.
P2 / High
Team blocked; no practical alternative; dispatch deadline within one hour.
P3 / Normal
One person or workstation; a usable alternative; no immediate team-wide
block.
01
Inspect the effect on work
Who is affected? What is blocked? Is there an alternative? When is the deadline?
02
Match the article to the symptom
KB-101: wrong printer. KB-202: online label printer with a stalled queue. KB-303: offline or
disconnected device.
03
Keep the action within its approved scope
Use the correct workstation, device, and job IDs. Never purge unrelated queues or reset a
shared server to solve this ticket.
04
Verify after the latest action
Confirm the right printer, readable output, and restored work. A new action invalidates the
earlier confirmation.
05
Document, then choose the status
Record evidence, action/KB, verification, and outcome/owner. Select Resolved only after
confirmation; otherwise retain ownership and use the appropriate waiting status.
