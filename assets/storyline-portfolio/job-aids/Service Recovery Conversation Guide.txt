ALDER & ASH / CONVERSATION GUIDE
A clear plan.
A promise you can keep.
Acknowledge the impact. Check the facts. Own the next update.
01
Name the specific impact
“You planned around that delivery. We set an expectation we could not verify.” Connect the
acknowledgment to the customer’s situation.
02
Read the evidence and confirm the need
Check the latest scan and earlier service note. Ask about the deadline and preference. An
earlier reassurance is not a confirmed delivery date.
03
Offer an authorized next step
For this confirmed delay: open a carrier trace and offer the $12 shipping refund. A trace
requests information; it does not guarantee arrival.
04
Request review and retain ownership
A team lead must review the $40 request. Explain that review is not approval. Jordan still owns
the next customer update.
05
Say who, how, and when
“I’m Jordan. I’ll email you by 4 p.m. today, even if the carrier or lead has not replied.”
Summarize the actions and any remaining uncertainty.
Made an inaccurate promise? Name it. Replace it. Own it.
“I need to correct what I said: I cannot guarantee Friday delivery. The latest scan has no confirmed date.
I’ll open a trace and email you by four.” Correct an approval claim just as explicitly: $40 requires lead
review.
