# Workplace feedback: functional review

Review date: 2026-09-10.

## What was checked

- All 27 combinations of the three choices at each of three decisions reached a review containing three route notes.
- Each choice revealed Alex’s response and feedback, then enabled the next action.
- Restart returned to the opening decision after every completed route.
- One complete route and restart were exercised using Tab, Space, and Enter, checking focus at each step.
- The case page loaded its images without horizontal overflow at viewport widths of 1440, 390, and 320 pixels.
- An axe-core automated scan of the initial case page reported no violations of the selected WCAG 2 A/AA and WCAG 2.1 AA rules.

## Scope and limits

These were automated browser checks in Chromium, not participant sessions. Keyboard checks cover one complete route; the 27-combination check used pointer activation. The automated accessibility scan covered the initial case page, not every feedback state or screen-reader behavior. The checks do not establish WCAG conformance or learning effectiveness.

No learner or workplace outcome is reported. A separate pilot protocol specifies the transfer task, rubric, observation record, and revision process.
