HIT YOUR MARK - INTRODUCTION (about 1 minute)

Three. Two. One. Hit your mark.

On stage, a performer walks toward a tape mark on the floor.

Every second, the distance to the mark shrinks.

In the pool, a swimmer pushes off the wall and glides.

Every second, the distance swum grows.

One number goes down. One goes up.

But both of them are lines.

The slope is the rate: how much it changes each second.

The intercept is the start: where you are when the clock begins.

Change either one, and the whole line moves.

Now here's the problem.

The director moves the cue earlier.

The coach wants a faster split.

Keep the old plan, and you miss.

Early is wrong. Late is wrong.

So what do you change: the rate, or the start?

Choose your scene. Build the model. Graph it, adjust it, and hit your mark.

VISUAL DESCRIPTION
A countdown opens on a stage. A performer walks toward a tape mark on a wooden stage floor while a stopwatch runs and a bar showing the distance to the mark shrinks. A swimmer pushes off a pool wall and glides while a second bar showing distance swum grows. A split screen shows one arrow pointing down for the stage and one pointing up for the pool. Both situations become straight lines on one time-and-distance graph. The word SLOPE appears, then a step triangle on a line labeled "= the rate". The word INTERCEPT appears, then a dot where the line meets the vertical axis labeled "= where you start", with a stopwatch at zero. A line pivots (change the rate) and slides (change the start). Then the problem: a clock hand jumps to an earlier cue on stage, and a stopwatch reads "faster" in the pool. The old plan's line passes a dashed "new goal" line and is stamped MISS. A token reaches the mark before the clock (EARLY), then after it (LATE). The questions "The rate?" and "Or the start?" appear over moving lines. A stage and pool split screen invites the viewer to choose a scene; the words BUILD, GRAPH and ADJUST flash over a line; the video ends on the stage's tape mark with the title HIT YOUR MARK. No numbers or answers are given.
