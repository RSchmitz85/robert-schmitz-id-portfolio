HIT YOUR MARK - LESSON HOOK

You're heading for your mark.

You know the distance.

You know the pace.

Then the cue changes.

Early won't work.

Late won't work.

Can you change the plan... and arrive exactly on time?

VISUAL DESCRIPTION
An illustrated performer approaches a stage mark, followed by a swimmer approaching a pool wall. Distance and pace are posed as known information without revealing quantities. A cue changes. Two abstract timeline diagrams contrast early and late arrivals. The final screen invites the learner to choose the pool or stage challenge. Grok motion is introductory footage; exact numerical models appear only later in the interactive lesson.
