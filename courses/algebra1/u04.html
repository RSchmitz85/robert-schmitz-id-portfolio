Algebra 1 / Unit 4: Functions and representations

Unit 4: Functions and representations

Essential question

What makes a relationship a function, and what do its inputs mean?

Learning targets

  • Determine whether every input has exactly one output.
  • Evaluate and interpret f(x) in context.
  • Distinguish mathematical domains from contextual restrictions.
  • Describe intercepts, intervals of change, and average rate.

Lesson sequence

  1. Recognize a function
  2. Use function notation
  3. Choose a meaningful domain
  4. Read change from representations

Apply and check your learning

Take the unit self-check · Complete the application task

Plan your time

Suggested unit allocation: about 3 weeks, adjusted after the readiness check and classroom evidence. The four core lessons are starting points for instruction; use additional meetings for discussion, spaced practice, reteaching, the application task, and revision.
