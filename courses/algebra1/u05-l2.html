5.2 · Graph slope-intercept form

Learning target

Graph y = mx + b using an intercept and a rate.

Think about the idea

In y = mx + b, b is the y value when x = 0, and m is slope. Plot (0, b), then use the signed rise and run to locate another point. A line needs a consistent scale and labeled axes. Check a plotted point by substituting it into the equation.

Worked example

Graph y = −2x + 3.

  1. Plot the y-intercept (0, 3).
  2. Slope −2 = −2/1 means move right 1 and down 2 to (1, 1).
  3. Another point is (2, −1). Connect these points for a real-number domain.

Spot and repair an error

A learner uses (3, 0) as the y-intercept.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

The y-axis has x = 0, so the y-intercept is (0, 3).

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Give two points on y = 3x − 1.

Check the reasoning

(0, −1) and (1, 2), obtained by substitution.

Practice 2

What is the slope and y-intercept of y = 5 − x?

Check the reasoning

Slope −1; y-intercept (0, 5).

Practice 3

Does (2, 7) lie on y = 2x + 3?

Check the reasoning

Yes: 7 = 2(2) + 3.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
