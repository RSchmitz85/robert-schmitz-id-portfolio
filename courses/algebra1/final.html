Cumulative Algebra 1 self-check

This is a self-check. Write your reasoning before choosing an answer. Feedback appears after checking; this public preview does not save grades.

1. A service charges $11 once and $6 per visit. Cost for 4 visits?

11 + 6(4) = 35.

2. Solve 3(x − 1) = 2x + 5.

3x−3=2x+5 gives x=8.

3. Solve −5x < 20.

Divide by −5 and reverse direction.

4. If f(x)=x²+1, what is f(−3)?

(−3)²+1=9+1=10.

5. A line has slope 4 and passes through (2, 3). What is its equation?

3=4(2)+b gives b=−5.

6. Solve x+y=11 and x−y=5.

Adding gives 2x=16, then y=3.

7. Which models a starting amount 250 declining 8% each year?

A decline of 8% retains 92% per year.

8. Factor x²−9x+20.

−4 and −5 sum to −9 and multiply to 20.

9. Solve x²−6x+5=0.

(x−1)(x−5)=0, so x=1 or 5.

10. A model predicts 42 points and an observation is 47 points. Residual?

Observed minus predicted: 47−42=5 points.
