Algebra 1 / 1.1 · Read quantities and units

1.1 · Read quantities and units

Learning target

Use units to interpret and check a calculation.

Think about the idea

A quantity combines a number with a unit. Unit rates compare quantities per one unit. Track units as you multiply or divide; an answer with the wrong unit cannot answer the question. Convert measurements before combining them.

Worked example

A cyclist travels 18 miles in 1.5 hours. Find the average speed.

  1. Average speed = distance ÷ time.
  2. 18 miles ÷ 1.5 hours = 12 miles per hour.
  3. Check: 12 miles/hour × 1.5 hours = 18 miles.

Spot and repair an error

A learner says 18 × 1.5 = 27 miles per hour.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

Multiplication gives mile-hours here. Divide miles by hours to obtain miles per hour.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Convert 2.5 hours to minutes.

Check the reasoning

150 minutes: multiply 2.5 by 60 minutes/hour.

Practice 2

A printer makes 84 pages in 3 minutes. Find its rate.

Check the reasoning

28 pages per minute: 84 ÷ 3 = 28.

Practice 3

At $4 per kilogram, what does 2.5 kilograms cost?

Check the reasoning

$10: $4/kg × 2.5 kg = $10.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
