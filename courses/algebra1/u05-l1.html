Algebra 1 / 5.1 · Connect slope to rate

5.1 · Connect slope to rate

Learning target

Calculate slope and interpret its sign and units.

Think about the idea

Slope compares vertical change with horizontal change: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). Keep the subtraction order consistent. A positive slope rises, a negative slope falls, and zero slope is horizontal. Vertical lines have undefined slope because their horizontal change is zero.

Worked example

Find the slope through (−1, 2) and (3, 10).

  1. Vertical change = 10 − 2 = 8.
  2. Horizontal change = 3 − (−1) = 4.
  3. Slope = 8/4 = 2. The output increases 2 units per input unit.

Spot and repair an error

A learner reverses only the denominator and obtains −2.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

Reverse both differences or neither. The same point must come first in numerator and denominator.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Find slope through (0, 5) and (2, 1).

Check the reasoning

−2: (1 − 5)/(2 − 0) = −4/2.

Practice 2

Find slope through (−2, 4) and (3, 4).

Check the reasoning

0: the vertical change is zero.

Practice 3

Find slope through (2, 1) and (2, 6).

Check the reasoning

Undefined: the denominator is 2 − 2 = 0.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
