Unit 6 self-check · Systems and shared solutions

This is a self-check. Write your reasoning before choosing an answer. Feedback appears after checking; this public preview does not save grades.

1. Solve y = 3x and x + y = 16.

x + 3x = 16 gives x = 4 and y = 12.

2. Solve x + y = 9 and x − y = 3.

Adding yields 2x = 12, then y = 3.

3. Classify y = 2x + 3 and 2y = 4x + 6.

Divide the second equation by 2 to obtain the first.

4. Which point satisfies x + y ≤ 5 and y ≥ x?

(1,3) satisfies 4 ≤ 5 and 3 ≥ 1. Each other point violates a constraint.

5. Six tickets at $4 or $7 total $30. How many $7 tickets?

Let b count $7 tickets: 4(6−b)+7b=30, so 3b=6 and b=2.
