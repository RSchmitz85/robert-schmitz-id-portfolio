2.4 · Rearrange formulas and model

Learning target

Isolate a variable in a formula and state restrictions.

Think about the idea

A formula connects quantities; solving for a variable makes that quantity explicit. Use the same balance rules as with numerical equations. If you divide by an expression, state that it must be nonzero. Interpret the result with units.

Worked example

Solve A = bh/2 for h.

  1. Multiply both sides by 2: 2A = bh.
  2. Divide by b, assuming b ≠ 0: h = 2A/b.
  3. For A = 30 square centimeters and b = 10 centimeters, h = 6 centimeters.

Spot and repair an error

A learner writes h = A/(2b).

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

Multiplying by 2 undoes division by 2. Substitute the proposed formula into A = bh/2 to expose the error.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Solve P = 2l + 2w for l.

Check the reasoning

l = (P − 2w)/2 = P/2 − w.

Practice 2

Solve d = rt for t.

Check the reasoning

t = d/r for r ≠ 0; distance divided by speed gives time.

Practice 3

A $9 fee plus $4 per visit totals $37. How many visits?

Check the reasoning

9 + 4v = 37, so v = 7 visits; 9 + 28 = 37.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
