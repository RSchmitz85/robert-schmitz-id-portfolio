Algebra 1 / 10.1 · Describe distributions

10.1 · Describe distributions

Learning target

Compare center and spread and consider outliers.

Think about the idea

The mean uses every value; the median is the middle of sorted data. The range measures overall spread, while the interquartile range describes the middle half. A box plot summarizes five numbers. Outliers can pull the mean more than the median, so compare distributions in context.

Worked example

For 2, 3, 4, 5, 16, compare mean and median.

  1. The sum is 30, so the mean is 30/5=6.
  2. The sorted middle value is 4, so the median is 4.
  3. The high value 16 pulls the mean upward. The range is 16−2=14.

Spot and repair an error

A learner computes range as 16 + 2.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

Range is a difference: maximum minus minimum. It measures distance between the extremes.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Find mean and median of 3, 5, 7, 9.

Check the reasoning

Mean 6; median (5+7)/2=6.

Practice 2

Find the range of 4, 4, 6, 11.

Check the reasoning

7: 11−4.

Practice 3

Using medians of the lower and upper halves excluding the overall median, find Q1 and Q3 for 1,2,3,4,5,6,7.

Check the reasoning

Q1=2 and Q3=6, so IQR=4. The stated convention excludes the overall median 4.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
