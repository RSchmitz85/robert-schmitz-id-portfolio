6.2 · Solve by substitution

Learning target

Substitute an equivalent expression to solve a system.

Think about the idea

When an equation already isolates a variable, replace that variable in the other equation with the equal expression. Use parentheses to preserve grouped operations. Solve the resulting one-variable equation, then substitute back to find the other variable.

Worked example

Solve y = 2x + 1 and x + y = 10.

  1. Replace y: x + (2x + 1) = 10.
  2. 3x + 1 = 10 gives x = 3.
  3. Then y = 2(3) + 1 = 7; check 3 + 7 = 10.

Spot and repair an error

A learner replaces y but leaves another y in the same substituted equation.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

The purpose is one equation in one variable. Replace every occurrence of the isolated variable in that equation.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Solve y = x + 4 and 2x + y = 13.

Check the reasoning

(3, 7): 3x + 4 = 13 gives x = 3 and y = 7.

Practice 2

Solve x = 2y and x + y = 12.

Check the reasoning

(8, 4): 3y = 12, then x = 8.

Practice 3

Solve y = 5 − x and y = x + 1.

Check the reasoning

(2, 3): 5 − x = x + 1 gives x = 2.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
