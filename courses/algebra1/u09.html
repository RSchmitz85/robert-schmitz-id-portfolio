Unit 9: Quadratics and nonlinear models

Essential question

Which form of a quadratic makes the question easiest to answer?

Learning targets

  • Interpret vertex form, symmetry, and intercepts.
  • Apply the zero-product property and square-root method.
  • Choose a method to solve a quadratic and classify real roots.
  • Interpret a quadratic model and choose feasible solutions.

Lesson sequence

  1. Read a parabola
  2. Solve using factors and roots
  3. Complete the square and use the formula
  4. Use a quadratic model

Apply and check your learning

Take the unit self-check · Complete the application task

Plan your time

Suggested unit allocation: about 3 weeks, adjusted after the readiness check and classroom evidence. The four core lessons are starting points for instruction; use additional meetings for discussion, spaced practice, reteaching, the application task, and revision.
