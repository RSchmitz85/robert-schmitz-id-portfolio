Algebra 1 / Unit 10: Data, evidence, and modeling

Unit 10: Data, evidence, and modeling

Essential question

How can a model help us explain data without claiming too much?

Learning targets

  • Compare center and spread and consider outliers.
  • Compute joint and conditional relative frequencies.
  • Interpret a fitted line and compute a residual.
  • Compare linear, exponential, and quadratic patterns and communicate limits.

Lesson sequence

  1. Describe distributions
  2. Read two-way tables
  3. Fit and critique linear models
  4. Choose and defend a model

Apply and check your learning

Take the unit self-check · Complete the application task

Plan your time

Suggested unit allocation: about 3 weeks, adjusted after the readiness check and classroom evidence. The four core lessons are starting points for instruction; use additional meetings for discussion, spaced practice, reteaching, the application task, and revision.
