Algebra 1 / Unit 2: Equations and mathematical arguments

Unit 2: Equations and mathematical arguments

Essential question

How do we know that a solution makes an equation true?

Learning targets

  • Solve a two-step equation and verify the result.
  • Solve equations with distribution and variables on both sides.
  • Distinguish one solution, no solution, and all real numbers.
  • Isolate a variable in a formula and state restrictions.

Lesson sequence

  1. Keep an equation balanced
  2. Solve on both sides
  3. Identify special solution sets
  4. Rearrange formulas and model

Apply and check your learning

Take the unit self-check · Complete the application task

Plan your time

Suggested unit allocation: about 3 weeks, adjusted after the readiness check and classroom evidence. The four core lessons are starting points for instruction; use additional meetings for discussion, spaced practice, reteaching, the application task, and revision.
