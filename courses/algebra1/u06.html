Algebra 1 / Unit 6: Systems and shared solutions

Unit 6: Systems and shared solutions

Essential question

What does it mean to satisfy two relationships at once?

Learning targets

  • Identify a solution that satisfies both equations.
  • Substitute an equivalent expression to solve a system.
  • Add equivalent equations to eliminate one variable.
  • Use simultaneous equations or inequalities to model constraints.

Lesson sequence

  1. Interpret intersections
  2. Solve by substitution
  3. Solve by elimination
  4. Model systems and regions

Apply and check your learning

Take the unit self-check · Complete the application task

Plan your time

Suggested unit allocation: about 3 weeks, adjusted after the readiness check and classroom evidence. The four core lessons are starting points for instruction; use additional meetings for discussion, spaced practice, reteaching, the application task, and revision.
