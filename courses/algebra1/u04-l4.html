Algebra 1 / 4.4 · Read change from representations

4.4 · Read change from representations

Learning target

Describe intercepts, intervals of change, and average rate.

Think about the idea

An intercept occurs where a graph meets an axis. Increasing means outputs rise as inputs rise over an interval. Average rate of change is change in output divided by change in input. A table shows selected values; do not assume behavior between them without a model.

Worked example

A tank model gives V(0) = 30, V(2) = 22, V(5) = 10 liters. Find the average rate from 0 to 5 minutes.

  1. Change in volume = 10 − 30 = −20 liters.
  2. Change in time = 5 − 0 = 5 minutes.
  3. Average rate = −20/5 = −4 liters per minute. The initial value is 30 liters.

Spot and repair an error

A learner divides 10 by 5 and calls 2 the rate of change.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

Use differences, not just the last output divided by the last input. The tank already held water at t = 0.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Find average rate from (1, 7) to (4, 16).

Check the reasoning

3 output units per input unit: (16 − 7)/(4 − 1) = 9/3.

Practice 2

What does a y-intercept of 12 mean for a cost model?

Check the reasoning

The cost is 12 units of currency when the input is zero.

Practice 3

A graph rises until x = 2, then falls. Where is its turning point?

Check the reasoning

At x = 2. The corresponding y value is needed to give the complete point.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
