Unit 8 self-check · Polynomials and factoring

This is a self-check. Write your reasoning before choosing an answer. Feedback appears after checking; this public preview does not save grades.

1. Simplify (2x² + x) − (x² − 3x + 2).

Reverse every sign in the subtracted polynomial, then combine.

2. Expand (x − 2)(x + 6).

The products give x² + 6x − 2x − 12.

3. Factor 4x² − 36 completely.

Remove 4, then factor x² − 9 as a difference of squares.

4. Factor x² + 2x − 15.

5 + (−3) = 2 and 5(−3) = −15.

5. Which equals (x + 4)²?

Both middle products are 4x, giving 8x in total.
