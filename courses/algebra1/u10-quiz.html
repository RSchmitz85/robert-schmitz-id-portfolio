Unit 10 self-check · Data, evidence, and modeling

This is a self-check. Write your reasoning before choosing an answer. Feedback appears after checking; this public preview does not save grades.

1. Mean of 2, 4, 6, 8?

The sum is 20, divided by four observations gives 5.

2. Among 25 respondents in a subgroup, 15 say yes. Conditional yes rate?

15/25 = 0.60 = 60%.

3. A model predicts 18 and the observed value is 14. Residual?

Residual = observed − predicted = 14−18 = −4.

4. Outputs are 2, 5, 10, 17 for equally spaced inputs. Which pattern is suggested?

First differences 3,5,7 have constant second difference 2.

5. A strong association between two variables establishes what?

Association alone cannot distinguish causation from confounding or other explanations.
