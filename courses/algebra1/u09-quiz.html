Unit 9 self-check · Quadratics and nonlinear models

This is a self-check. Write your reasoning before choosing an answer. Feedback appears after checking; this public preview does not save grades.

1. Vertex of y = (x − 4)² + 2?

The squared term is zero at x=4; the minimum output is 2.

2. Solve x² − 7x + 10 = 0.

(x−2)(x−5)=0 gives 2 and 5.

3. Solve (x+2)² = 9.

x+2=±3 gives x=1 or −5.

4. How many distinct real roots does x² − 4x + 4 = 0 have?

The discriminant is 16−16=0; (x−2)²=0 has one distinct root.

5. For h(t)=−2t²+12t, when does the ball return to height zero after launch?

Factor −2t(t−6)=0. The positive return time is 6 seconds.
