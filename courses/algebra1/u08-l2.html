Algebra 1 / 8.2 · Multiply polynomials

8.2 · Multiply polynomials

Learning target

Distribute to multiply binomials and check the result.

Think about the idea

Multiply each term of one polynomial by every term of the other. Products of like bases add exponents, while the resulting sum combines only like terms. An area model can track the four partial products of two binomials.

Worked example

Expand (x + 3)(x − 2).

  1. Partial products are x², −2x, 3x, and −6.
  2. Combine the middle terms: x² + x − 6.
  3. At x=4, the original product is 7×2=14 and the expansion is 16+4−6=14.

Spot and repair an error

A learner writes (x + 3)² = x² + 9.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

The two middle products are each 3x. The full square is x² + 6x + 9.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Expand (x + 2)(x + 5).

Check the reasoning

x² + 7x + 10.

Practice 2

Expand (x − 4)(x + 4).

Check the reasoning

x² − 16; the middle terms cancel.

Practice 3

Expand 2x(x² − 3x + 1).

Check the reasoning

2x³ − 6x² + 2x.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
