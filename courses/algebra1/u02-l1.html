2.1 · Keep an equation balanced

Learning target

Solve a two-step equation and verify the result.

Think about the idea

An equation states that two expressions have equal values. Applying the same reversible operation to both sides preserves its solutions. Undo addition before multiplication when a variable term has an added constant. Verify by substituting into the original equation.

Worked example

Solve 3x + 7 = 22.

  1. Subtract 7 from both sides: 3x = 15.
  2. Divide both sides by 3: x = 5.
  3. Check: 3(5) + 7 = 22, so 5 is a solution.

Spot and repair an error

A learner divides only the 3x term by 3 and writes x + 7 = 22.

Before revealing the explanation, identify the first incorrect step and propose a repair.

Compare your repair

Divide the entire side, or first subtract 7. Every term on both sides must be included in the operation.

Your turn

Work each question independently. Include a method or explanation, then compare your reasoning.

Practice 1

Solve 4x − 9 = 19.

Check the reasoning

x = 7: add 9, then divide by 4; 28 − 9 = 19.

Practice 2

Solve x/3 + 2 = 8.

Check the reasoning

x = 18: subtract 2, then multiply by 3.

Practice 3

Solve −2x + 5 = 17.

Check the reasoning

x = −6: −2x = 12; dividing by −2 gives −6.

Reflect before moving on

Explain one decision you made and how you checked it. If your answer changed after checking, record the original error and your corrected reasoning.
