Unit 1 self-check · Quantities and expressions

This is a self-check. Write your reasoning before choosing an answer. Feedback appears after checking; this public preview does not save grades.

1. A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours. What is its average speed?

Divide distance by time: 150 ÷ 3 = 50 miles/hour.

2. Evaluate x² − 2x at x = −4.

(−4)² − 2(−4) = 16 + 8 = 24.

3. Which expression equals −3(2x − 5)?

Distribute −3 to both terms, including the negative 5.

4. Simplify 7a − 2 + 3a + 6.

Combine variable terms to get 10a and constants to get 4.

5. A rental costs $18 plus $7 per hour h. Which expression gives its cost?

The fixed fee is paid once. Only the hourly rate multiplies h.
