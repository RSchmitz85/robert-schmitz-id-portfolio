Start here: how to learn in this course

Welcome to Algebra 1

You will use symbols, graphs, equations, and data to describe relationships and defend conclusions. This original course draft is for learners beginning Algebra 1, typically grades 8–9. It assumes arithmetic with signed numbers, fractions, and basic ratios.

Your learning routine

  1. Read the objective and make a first attempt.
  2. Work through the model, explaining why each step is allowed.
  3. Attempt the three practice questions before opening their explanations.
  4. Repair the misconception, then take the unit self-check.
  5. Complete the application task and revise using the rubric.

Materials and working expectations

Use paper or a digital notebook, a pencil, graph paper, and a basic calculator for numerical checks. Graphing technology is optional. Show the method before relying on a calculator result. Record units, define variables, and explain why a solution fits its context.

Feedback and support

Practice explanations are available for self-correction. If you miss two or more unit quiz questions, return to the related lessons, write an error correction, and try again. Ask your instructor to review your explanation when the method remains unclear. No time limits or dates are built into this draft.
