Unit 3 application · Inequalities and constraints

Your challenge

Plan a workshop with a $200 room fee and $12 of materials per participant. The budget is at most $500 and at least 10 people must attend. Write both constraints, combine them, identify the feasible whole-number participant counts, and defend the largest group.

What to submit

  • A clearly labeled model with defined variables and units.
  • Your calculations and any requested tables or graphs.
  • A check of the result and a short explanation of assumptions or limitations.
  • A revision note explaining one check or change you made.

Submit typed work or a legible scan/photo through the Canvas assignment after import. In this preview, use paper or a document; no work is uploaded or saved here.
