Course map and pacing

A full-year planning structure

Suggested allocation: 2 weeks for orientation, readiness, and prerequisite review; 30 weeks across ten units; and 4 flexible weeks for cumulative practice, reteaching, application revisions, and synthesis. This is a 36-week planning draft, not a district pacing mandate. Forty core lessons need teacher-led practice and adaptation to fill a full instructional year.

Scope boundaries

The draft emphasizes Algebra 1 equations, inequalities, functions, linear and exponential change, polynomial structure, quadratics, and introductory statistics. It does not claim coverage of every high-school standard. Absolute-value and piecewise functions, more extensive transformations, regression technology, formal standard-deviation calculations, and additional graph-based investigations require local decisions and supplementary lessons.
