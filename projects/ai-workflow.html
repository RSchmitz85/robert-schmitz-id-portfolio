Design practice

AI-assisted drafting and review

I use AI to generate candidate outlines, explanations, and distractors, then evaluate those candidates against the learning objective and the mathematics.

Process note · Review criteria and workflow

Context

My responsibility

The working sequence

  1. Define the task

    Name the audience, learning objective, required response, and constraints before requesting a draft.

  2. Generate candidates

    Use drafting assistance for outlines, alternate explanations, or distractor ideas. Keep candidate language separate from the final materials.

  3. Check the reasoning

    Solve the item independently, test each distractor, and check that the explanation preserves the intended mathematics.

  4. Revise and inspect

    Rewrite accepted language for the learner, integrate it into the material, and inspect the rendered page or interaction.

What a candidate must pass

  • Accuracy and alignmentEvery worked step must be valid and the task must measure the stated objective. Distractors should represent a specific plausible error.
  • Useful feedbackThe message should identify a decision the learner can revisit. A longer restatement of the answer does not necessarily help.
  • Reading and presentationCheck vocabulary, density, notation, contrast, and the relationship between the text and graphic.
  • Appropriate inputUse the information needed for the design task; keep identifiable learner information out of drafting prompts.
