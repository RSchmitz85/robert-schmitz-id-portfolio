Background

Learning design grounded in teaching.

I’m Robert Schmitz, an instructional designer and curriculum developer with a background in secondary and adult mathematics education.

How I approach a learning problem

I start with the performance the learner needs to demonstrate. Then I plan the practice, feedback, and evidence of understanding. Teaching mathematics has made me attentive to the small decision that changes a solution: a sign, a grouping choice, or a mismatch between a graph and its context.

That perspective carries into my curriculum and assessment design. I build student materials alongside the teacher guidance that makes the intended reasoning clear.

The work you can inspect

Real Engagement with Mathematics (REM) is my independent curriculum work. This portfolio shows representative lesson materials, an annotated assessment task, a Storyline interaction specification, and a professional-development design sample. Each page distinguishes the available artifact from the work still to be built or evaluated.

My district teaching, curriculum, assessment, and LMS experience informs these design choices. The public REM samples have their own provenance; they are not evidence of a district implementation.
